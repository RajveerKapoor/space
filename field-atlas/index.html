Evidence behind the scene

The 11.405-day Doppler signal appears in 233 nights of CARMENES data. Its period matches Teegarden’s Star c, first reported in 2019. The estimated minimum mass is 1.09 Earth masses. The true mass depends on orbital inclination.

Our circular fit gives 0.0456 AU and 0.53 times Earth’s irradiation. A suitable atmosphere could allow surface liquid water. The surface, atmosphere and orbital orientations shown here are artistic choices.

The star map uses equatorial coordinates and distances from the NASA Exoplanet Archive. The orbit view uses catalog semimajor axes for all three planets. Their displayed sizes and phases are illustrative.
